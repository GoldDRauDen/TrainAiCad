# 521026-13 / 26-063295 — the step S gates the bend-end relief at every angle (user DUYỆT 2026-10-26)

Rule: `rules/SLIT_RELIEF.md` §3–4; executable `tools/lookup.py slit`, `tools/bend_corner.py`, `tools/bend_end_guard.py`, `tests/test_non90_tabs.py`.

## FACT
- 26-063295 (SPHCP t6, KH851): tabs 38 mm at both ends bent 120° out of an 820 plate, Nobi 4.92 handwritten. The user's HD sample has the web edge between the tabs at y65, the bend lines at 67.01 / 71.93: B is before the zone, S = +6.93 → no slit.
- The job drew a Green slit D = t·sin120 = 5.196 at both corners, with its own site record saying S = +4.92.

## ROOT_CAUSE
`AI_RULE_MISAPPLICATION` + `AI_RULE_GAP`: the schema said the 90° S decision "is not applied" to NON_90_RELIEF, `lookup.py slit --angle` returned a depth without asking for S, and `bend_corner` refused any step at a non-90° bend, so the part was patched by hand. The 90° gate (S>0: nothing) is the gate at every angle; the angle only changes the depth.

## LESSON
S>0 → no relief at any angle. S ≤ 0 → slit from B, depth `t·sin θ` past the flange-side bend line (`|S| + t·sin θ` from B), lead and R0.5 as at 90°. `NON_90_RELIEF` is measured like `BEND_END_OFFSET_90` (decision `NO_RELIEF` / `SLIT`), never a free `SEPARATE_RULE`.
